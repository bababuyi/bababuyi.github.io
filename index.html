About

Game Programmer.

Yellooo. My name is Baraka, a game programmer and lifelong game nerd born and raised in Harare, Zimbabwe. Since graduating from Asia Pacific University (dual degree with De Montfort University) I have shipped a commissioned game for a client, released two jam games, and I currently work as a contract programmer on Blind Spot, a game for the GPF Foundation.

I enjoy programming systems in both Unreal Engine and Unity, dabble in puzzle mechanics, and am working on pushing my AI programming further. I do game design in my own projects and jump in to help others with theirs when I get the chance. I can hold my own with 3D modelling up to hard surfaces, but I can't do organic things to save my life :<.

Fun fact: I speak 3 languages and understand 4. I was an exchange student in Brazil, and somewhere along the way I picked up Spanish by accident. I'd speak Portuguese to my Mexican friends and they'd reply in Spanish, and it just clicked. Então, se você vier falar comigo em português, eu agradeceria muito.

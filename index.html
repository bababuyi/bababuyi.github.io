About

Game Programmer.

Yellooo. My name is Baraka, a fresh graduate from Asia Pacific University (dual degree with De Montfort University) and lifelong game nerd born and raised in Harare, Zimbabwe.

I enjoy programming systems in both Unreal Engine and Unity, dabble in puzzle mechanics, and working on pushing my AI programming further. I can hold my own with 3D modelling up to hard surfaces, I can't do organic things to save my life :<. I do game design in my own projects and jump in to help others with theirs when I get the chance.

Fun fact: I speak 3 languages and understand 4. I was an exchange student in Brazil, and somewhere along the way I picked up Spanish by accident — I'd speak Portuguese to my Mexican friends and they'd reply in Spanish, and it just clicked. Então, se você vier falar comigo em português, eu agradeceria muito.
